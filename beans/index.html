Know Your Roast

🌱

Light Roast

Bright acidity, floral notes, and a tea-like body. Best for pour-over and origin tasting.

☕

Medium Roast

The everyday hero — balanced sweetness, smooth finish. Works for everything from drip to French press.

🖤

Dark Roast

Bold, chocolatey, and smoky. Rich crema for espresso, or a heavy-bodied French press cup.

🌍

Ethiopian Single-Origin

Wild, citrusy, and unforgettable. The roast that started Maya's 500-pound emergency. Taste the chaos.
