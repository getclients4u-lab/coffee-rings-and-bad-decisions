Meet the Cast

The full line-up, in their own words (sort of).

☕

Maya Chen

The Protagonist

29 · Owner of Coffee Rings, the struggling specialty shop on a rainy Portland corner. Sharp, stubborn, and secretly a hopeless romantic. Runs on cold brew and even worse decisions.

Signature move

Orders espresso with the lid off so she can inhale it faster between disasters.

The arc

Closed-off → vulnerable. Her whole journey is learning to ask for help instead of fighting everything alone.

🤵

Leo Bianchi

The Suit

32 · Acquisitions director at Bean & Leaf — the very chain trying to put Maya out of business. Charming, understated, and quietly allergic to being the bad guy.

Signature move

Grew up in a family restaurant, so he knows exactly what losing a business feels like.

The arc

People-pleaser → assertive. Learns to stop playing it safe and fight for something — and someone — that matters.

🔒 SpoilerAfter the viral festival disaster, he's fired — then shows up at Maya's shop, wet and unemployed, because it's the only place that feels like home.

🪄

Sam

The Best Friend

28 · Maya's best friend and head barista. Full-time chaos engine with wingman energy strong enough to power the espresso machine, plus a side hustle selling vintage mugs.

Signature move

Masterminds the "Mystery Bean Lottery" and every conspiracy theory about Bean & Leaf.

👵

Mrs. Hsu

The RegularThe Landlord

72 · The quiet regular at the corner table. Orders the same thing every single day, tips in exact change, and notices absolutely everything.

Signature move

Leaves passive-aggressive notes about the noise, then slips you the rent money you're short.

🔒 SpoilerShe owns the building. Maya's silent guardian-angel landlord has been looking out for her the whole time.

📱

Jake

The Ex

27 · Maya's ex. An influencer who tried to turn her shop into a content farm, and learned the hard way that "going viral" is not a business plan.

Signature move

Every crisis in his life comes with its own hashtag.

🔒 SpoilerReturns begging for a second chance — but by then he's just a guy who needs to become a real customer.

🦈

Rachel

The Shark

31 · Leo's boss and a shark in a blazer. Wants to acquire Maya's shop at any cost, and doesn't much care who she steamrolls on the way.

Signature move

Pitches a hostile buyout the way other people offer condolences.

🏢

Bean & Leaf

The Chain

The corporate chain trying to put Coffee Rings out of business. Gleaming, efficient, and just a little too proud of its forty-seven seasonal syrups.

Signature move

Its "small-batch" menu has forty-seven items and none of them are small.

🔒 SpoilerBy the end, redrawn as a smaller, more ethical buyer who actually cares about coffee.
