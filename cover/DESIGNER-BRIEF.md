# Coffee Rings and Bad Decisions — Designer Cover Brief
**Author:** Alex Rogers  
**Genre:** Romantic Comedy  
**Series:** Book 1 of The Occupied Series  
**Trim:** 6×9 print / 2560×1600 eBook landscape  
**Target:** Amazon KDP paperback + Kindle

---

## Exact Reference Style
Match the aesthetic of the sample images provided:
- Warm golden-hour coffee shop photography
- City skyline at dusk in background
- Shallow depth of field, bokeh lights
- Pink/gold/white/cream palette on dark navy-to-pink gradient sky

---

## Typography Hierarchy (Exact)

### Top Zone (~5-8% from top edge)
- **Text:** `A ROMANTIC COMEDY`
- **Font:** Small, clean, pink sans-serif all-caps
- **Treatment:** Centered, flanked by small outlined hearts on both sides
- **Size:** Approx 1.5-2% of cover height

### Title Block (Upper 35-40% of cover)
- **Line 1:** `Coffee Rings`
  - Font: Large elegant white brush/script serif
  - Color: Cream/white
  - Effect: Subtle drop shadow for contrast
  - Size: Dominant text, ~12-14% of cover height
- **Line 2:** `and`
  - Font: Tiny orange/coral cursive script connector
  - Position: Centered between "Rings" and "Bad"
  - Size: ~3% of cover height
- **Line 3:** `Bad Decisions`
  - Font: Large vibrant hot-pink brush script
  - Size: Same as "Coffee Rings", ~12-14% of cover height
  - Effect: Slight sweep/diagonal energy, not rigid

### Middle Zone (40-75% from top)
- **Visual focus:** Coffee cup as central hero prop
- **Optional accent:** Large translucent gold coffee ring stain graphic on right side of title block

### Bottom Zone (Last 20% of cover)
- **Author:** `ALEX ROGERS`
  - Font: Bold white spaced-out capital letters
  - Flanked by small pink hearts on left and right
  - Size: ~6-7% of cover height
- **Series:** `BOOK 1 OF THE OCCUPIED SERIES`
  - Font: Tiny white sans-serif all-caps
  - Position: Directly under author name
  - Size: ~2% of cover height

## Color Palette

- **Primary:** Deep navy blue (sky top) → purple → pink/orange (horizon)
- **Text:** White/cream, vibrant hot pink, small orange accents
- **Props:** White mug, yellow note, pink note, gold coffee ring, tortoiseshell brown
- **Lighting:** Warm golden, romantic, inviting

---

## Layout Rules & Safe Areas

- All text centered horizontally
- Top margin: 5-8% of total height
- Title block: upper 35-40%
- Coffee cup visual center: ~55% down from top
- Author name: ~10% from bottom edge
- Minimum 0.25" safe zone inside trim for all text
- No text on spine for eBook; print spine: "Coffee Rings and Bad Decisions — Alex Rogers"

---

## What NOT To Include

- NO garbled text, no AI artifact words
- NO chalkboard text in background
- NO watermarks or logos
- NO text overlapping props
- NO dark thriller-style bands (this is warm rom-com)
- NO extra words below author name

---

## KDP File Specs

### eBook Cover
- Dimensions: 2560×1600 pixels
- Aspect ratio: 1.6:1
- Color: RGB
- Resolution: ~300 DPI
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- File size: Under 20MB

### Print Full-Wrap Cover (PDF)
- Trim: 6×9 inches
- Bleed: 0.125" on all sides
- Safe margin: 0.25" inside trim
- Spine width: ~0.48" for 203 pages standard white paper
- Back cover: blurb + author bio + small headshot space
- No crop marks
- Flatten all transparencies
- RGB color

## Book Context (For Designer Reference)

**Logline:** Maya Chen runs a Portland coffee shop. A midnight order triggers a 500-pound bean emergency. She has three weeks to sell it before her loan defaults. The only person who can help is Leo Bianchi — the corporate suit she publicly humiliated.

**Tone:** Warm, witty, chaotic, romantic. Think: *The Hating Game* meets *You've Got Mail* with coffee.

**Key themes:** Small business, coffee culture, reluctant partnership, viral TikTok, found family.
